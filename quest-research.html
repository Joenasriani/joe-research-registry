Quest Research

A research series by Joe Nasr / QuestRequestVR exploring Meta Quest engineering, XR and VR, mixed reality, spatial computing, human perception, multimodal interfaces, simulation and interactive technical visualization.

Author: Joe Nasr · Research registry: Joe Nasr Research Registry · Project identity: QuestRequestVR

Who this research is useful for

These projects are written and structured for people who build, teach, test or investigate emerging interfaces: AI builders, tech builders, AI creators, AI teachers, Meta Quest engineers, XR and VR developers, spatial-computing developers, creative technologists, HCI researchers, simulation developers, robotics and sensing experimenters, technical educators, prototypers and VR enthusiasts.

The collection focuses on concrete technical questions at the intersection of software, sensors, hardware, immersive interfaces and human reasoning—not generic claims that a project is “AI-powered.”

Projects

Technical research / design study

Quest-XSA — Thermal & UV Sensory Augmentation for Meta Quest

Explores how external thermal and ultraviolet sensors could become real-time mixed-reality information channels on Meta Quest 3. The project connects XR engineering, external sensing, Android/Unity integration, shaders, spatial interfaces and augmented human perception.

Relevant frames: Meta Quest engineering, sensory augmentation, multimodal interfaces, thermal imaging, UV imaging, HCI, robotics sensing, spatial computing.

Systems concept / feasibility study

Quest-MFTS — Multi-Function Tactical Simulation System for Meta Quest

Investigates a multimodal immersive simulation architecture combining headset display, gaze, voice, HOTAS, haptics, OpenXR and mixed-reality interfaces. The emphasis is systems integration and interface design rather than claiming equivalence with purpose-built military systems.

Relevant frames: VR simulation, mixed reality, gaze interfaces, speech interaction, haptics, multimodal control, human-machine interfaces, immersive training.

Interactive research explainer

Quest-4D — Simulating Hyperspace: 4D Perception in VR

An interactive visualization study asking how software can help people reason about higher-dimensional geometry through projection, transformation and direct manipulation. It is an educational and interface experiment, not a claim of literal human perception of a fourth spatial dimension.

Relevant frames: higher-dimensional visualization, spatial reasoning, interactive mathematics, VR education, scientific visualization, explainable interfaces.

Technical research / experimental systems study

Project EagleEye — Low-Cost Mixed-Reality Situational Awareness Research

Explores a consumer-hardware architecture combining Meta Quest 3, thermal sensing, LoRa/Meshtastic concepts, USB-C power/data and gaze-driven interaction. The research question is which selected situational-awareness functions can be prototyped economically with accessible hardware.

Relevant frames: mixed-reality sensing, thermal imaging, LoRa, wearable computing, multimodal interfaces, robotics, field visualization, spatial AI interfaces.

Research integrity

Each project should be read according to its stated status. A design architecture is not treated as a completed implementation; a feasibility study is not treated as an experimentally validated system; a visualization is not treated as physical proof; and project-reported measurements are not treated as independently reproduced results unless the method and evidence are published.

This distinction is intentional. The collection is meant to be useful to builders and researchers because it makes the boundary between implemented, proposed, tested and future work explicit.
