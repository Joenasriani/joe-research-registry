Who this collection is for

Meta Quest engineers, XR/VR developers, tech builders, spatial-computing developers, creative technologists, HCI researchers, simulation developers, robotics/sensing experimenters, technical educators, researchers and advanced VR enthusiasts.

Research integrity

A design architecture is not treated as a completed implementation. A feasibility study is not an experimentally validated system. A visualization is not physical proof. A narrative evidence review is not a systematic review unless it publishes the necessary reproducible methodology. Medical topics are not presented as clinical guidance.
