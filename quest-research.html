Quest Research

A research series by Joe Nasr / QuestRequestVR spanning Meta Quest engineering, XR and VR, mixed reality, spatial computing, sensory augmentation, simulation, wearable sensing, human-computer interaction and interactive technical visualization.

Author: Joe Nasr · Research registry: Joe Nasr Research Registry · Project identity: QuestRequestVR

Who this research is useful for

These projects are written and structured for people who build, teach, test or investigate emerging interfaces: Meta Quest engineers, XR and VR developers, tech builders, AI builders and creators, AI teachers, spatial-computing developers, creative technologists, HCI researchers, simulation developers, robotics and sensing experimenters, technical educators, prototypers and VR enthusiasts.

The collection uses AI-related framing only where it is technically relevant. The primary classification remains the actual engineering, HCI, visualization or simulation field of each project.

Research integrity

Each project should be read according to its stated status. A design architecture is not treated as a completed implementation; a feasibility study is not treated as an experimentally validated system; a visualization is not treated as physical proof; and project-reported measurements are not treated as independently reproduced results unless the method and evidence are published.
