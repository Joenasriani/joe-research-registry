Pre Institutional Human Order

If government and every other deliberately designed institution of collective order are removed from the model, what forms of human coordination would still arise from interaction itself?

Research on spontaneous order already asks how coordination can emerge without central design. Research on stateless societies shows that society without a state can still contain rules, authority, custom, and institutions. This note removes that wider institutional layer too, then asks what remains and what forms first.

Spontaneous order

Stateless societies

A society can lack a state while retaining kinship rules, custom, leadership, exchange, dispute settlement, and other institutions.

Social order without central authority

Relevant mechanisms include repeated interaction, reputation, reciprocity, communication, status, coalition formation, and local enforcement.
