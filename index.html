Pre Institutional Human Order

If government and every other deliberately designed institution of collective order are removed from the model, what forms of human coordination would still arise from interaction itself?

Research on spontaneous order already asks how coordination can emerge without central design. Research on stateless societies already shows that society without a state can still contain rules, authority, custom, and institutions. Note 001 removes that wider institutional layer too, then asks what remains and what forms first.

Closest research paths

Order without government

How can people coordinate when no state or central government directs them? This is an established question. Note 001 pushes the subtraction further.

Spontaneous order

How do practices, norms, and institutions arise from human action without being designed by a single directing authority?

Stateless societies

A society can lack a state while retaining kinship rules, custom, leadership, exchange, dispute settlement, and other institutions.

Social order without central authority

The relevant mechanisms include repeated interaction, reputation, reciprocity, communication, status, coalition formation, and local enforcement.
