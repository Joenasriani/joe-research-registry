01 / Role

Joe Nasr records research questions that begin with a specific conceptual problem, then tests their wording against existing scholarship before publishing them here.

The registry is not presented as a university journal, peer reviewed publication, or academic institution. Its purpose is to keep authorship, sources, revisions, and the boundary between an existing idea and a newly proposed framing visible.

02 / Current note

Research Note 001: Pre Institutional Human Order

The note asks what forms of human coordination remain when government and deliberately designed institutions are removed from the starting model.
